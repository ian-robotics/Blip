Blip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Blip is a student prototype built for a university game design course. It is a to-do app with a pixel pet. This page explains what information Blip uses and why.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Blip receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. Blip uses them only to show who is signed in and to keep each account's progress separate on your device. Blip does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data. You can also play as a guest without signing in.

Your tasks and progress

Your tasks, XP, pet and history are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not stored in an online account, and clearing your browser data removes them.

AI task features

When you describe tasks, or when Blip suggests tasks, the relevant task text is sent to Blip's server and to the Groq AI service to turn it into a task list or suggestions. This text is used only to produce that response. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in task descriptions.

What Blip does not do

  • Blip does not sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • Blip does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Blip does not send you email.

Removing your data

Use Reset demo in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser. You can revoke Blip's Google sign-in access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
